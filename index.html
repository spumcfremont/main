Four words we live by

The four I's are not a slogan. They're how the week is organized.

Each one names a real part of life at Saint Paul — and each color follows you through the rest of this page.

Inspiration

Blended worship, open communion, Bible study and prayer that send you back into Monday changed.

Incarnation

Faith with skin on it — small groups, family care, music, and people who show up when life is hard.

Intersection

Where church meets the neighborhood: dinner at the shelter every Friday, food, diapers, and advocacy.

Inclusion

Filipino, Caucasian, Indian, Ghanaian, all ages, every background. The table is Christ's, not ours.

New here?

What your first Sunday looks like

Park anywhere in the lot on Peace Terrace, walk in, and someone will find you. Dress however you're comfortable. Bring the kids — we're used to the noise.

  • Arrive 9:50 a.m. gives you time for coffee and a hello before we begin.
  • Communion Served every Sunday. Open to all — come forward or stay seated, either is fine.
  • Children Childcare for infants and toddlers; Kingdom Kidz for children through 6th grade.
  • Language Services are in English. Our congregation is majority Filipino, with families from across the Bay Area's many communities.

Who we are

Since 1964

Saint Paul UMC is a multi-cultural, multi-generational faith community called to make disciples of Jesus Christ for the transformation of the world. Roughly 250 people worship here each week, including 50–60 children.

We are part of the California-Nevada Annual Conference of The United Methodist Church.

    Our story

    Sixty years of moving, growing, and staying put

    Saint Paul has met in a storefront, a shared sanctuary, a historic chapel and an elementary school before finally building a home on Peace Terrace. The address changed a lot. The mission didn't.

    Today we are a congregation with a majority-Filipino membership worshiping alongside Caucasian, Asian Indian, Ghanaian and other families — still growing, still short on classroom space, still planning the Williams Ministry Center that will give our preschool and Kingdom Kidz room to expand.

    1963–1964

    A new church in Newark

    The California-Nevada Annual Conference starts a congregation; Saint Paul UMC is chartered in November 1964 with 84 members in a storefront on Newark Blvd.

    1973

    Finding our identity

    Rev. Maurice Cheek moves the congregation to a historic chapel at St. James Episcopal Church in Fremont.

    1984

    Land at last

    4.4 acres at Lowry Road and Alvarado Blvd purchased together with the Islamic Society of the East Bay.

    1990–1991

    Tri-City Christian Fellowship joins

    Ministry to the area's Filipino community becomes part of Saint Paul's life. Worship moves to Cabrillo Elementary.

    1994

    Home on Peace Terrace

    First worship service in the new sanctuary on Easter Sunday.

    2001

    Precious Time Preschool opens

    Founded by Deaconess Virginia Maniti Williams, still serving Fremont families today.

    2026

    Still here, still welcoming

    Worship at 10 a.m., dinner at the shelter on Fridays, and a preschool full of two-year-olds.
